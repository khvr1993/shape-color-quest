# Shape Color Quest ⭐

A friendly, big-tap game for **3–6 year olds** to learn shapes and colours, build attention, memory and listening skills.

## How to play
1. Tap **🔊 Hear it!** — the game says “Find the red star!”
2. Look at the big shapes on screen.
3. Tap the matching shape.
4. Earn ⭐ stars and points, build a streak 🔥, and collect a sticker 🎁 every 5 stars.

- **No punishment for mistakes** — wrong taps just wiggle and the game gently repeats the task.
- **Levels grow with your child:** Level 1 = 4 shapes, Level 2 = 6, Level 3 = 8.
- Distractors often share the *colour* or the *shape* of the target, so kids must look carefully.
- Voice prompts use your device’s text-to-speech. Sound and voice can be toggled in the top-right.

## Run it
Just open `index.html` in any browser — no build step, no dependencies, works offline on a tablet or phone.

## Why it's good for little learners
- Listening + looking + tapping links words to real shapes/colours
- Short rounds (one task at a time) suit a 5-year-old's attention span
- Praise, stars and stickers keep motivation positive

## Tech
Single self-contained `index.html` (HTML + CSS + JS, inline SVG shapes, Web Audio sounds).
